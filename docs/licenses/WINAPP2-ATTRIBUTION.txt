The "Deep clean" feature downloads and uses Winapp2.ini, a community-maintained
cleanup ruleset, at runtime. It is not bundled with ArchTweaker.

  Winapp2 — https://github.com/MoscaDotTo/Winapp2
  Licensed CC-BY-SA-4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

ArchTweaker uses only the file-cleaning (FileKey) rules and never the registry
rules. The downloaded copy is cached under C:\ProgramData\ArchTweaker\winapp2\.
